Redesign the homepage of a Japanese tax accounting firm "税理士法人 原澤会計" (Harasawa Tax & Accounting Office) located in Takasaki, Gunma Prefecture, Japan.

## Company Info
- Business: Tax accounting firm (税理士法人) serving corporate, individual, medical, and inheritance clients
- Location: Gunma Prefecture, Takasaki City
- Established: 2018 (Heisei 30)
- Key people: Representative Kazuhisa Harasawa, CPA Koichi Nagai
- Services: Tax advisory, MAS auditing, business planning, cloud accounting, startup support, inheritance planning, medical institution support

## Archetype: A (Corporate Formal)
Professional, trustworthy, authoritative. Clean lines, structured layout. Conveys reliability and expertise expected of a tax accounting firm.

## Color Palette
- Main: #19448e (deep navy blue — trust, professionalism)
- Sub: #96d04a (fresh green — growth, partnership)
- Accent: #E67E22 (warm orange for CTAs — approachable action)
- Background: #FFFFFF with subtle #F8F9FA sections
- Text: #333333

## Design Direction
- Modern Japanese corporate style with clean typography
- Hero section: Full-width image of the office building with overlay text
- Structured sections: About, News, Services (grid cards), Access (with map placeholder), Contact CTA
- Sticky header with logo and navigation
- Bottom sticky CTA bar for mobile
- Professional yet warm — a tax firm that feels approachable
- Use Noto Sans JP for Japanese text, Montserrat for English headings
- Responsive design targeting 375px to 1440px
- Service cards in a clean grid layout with icons
- Green accent line/bar elements for section dividers
- Navy blue for headers and primary buttons

## Sections (top to bottom)
1. Header: Logo + Nav (トップページ, 事業所案内, 新着情報, サービス内容, お問合せ)
2. Hero: Office building photo with tagline "個人・法人企業者様の良きパートナーとして"
3. About: Company introduction with description text
4. News: Recent updates (2 items)
5. Services: Grid of service categories (法人, 個人, 医療機関, 相続対策, 創業支援)
6. Access: Address, phone, email, map area
7. CTA: Contact section with phone and form buttons
8. Footer: Company info, nav links, copyright
